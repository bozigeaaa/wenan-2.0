# Fact-Check Rules

Fire-safety scripts are high-risk because professional audiences will notice fake standards, fake numbers, and exaggerated claims.

## High-Risk Facts

Treat these as high-risk. Use them only when confirmed by local files, official sources, test reports, or user confirmation:

- Exact SBC 801 clause numbers.
- Exact fire-resistance duration: 30/60/90/120 minutes, 2 hours, etc.
- Temperature claims such as 1000°C.
- Material density: kg/m3 values.
- Certification and test names: UL, ASTM E119, ASTM E84, ASTM E662, SASO, SABER, etc.
- Civil Defense outcomes: stop-work order, demolition, rejection, NCR, penalties.
- Absolute prohibition, universal applicability, perfect-coverage, or no-exception claims.
- Competitor prevalence, market-wide practice, exact failure-time, or categorical non-performance claims.
- Sole-answer or sole-compliant-solution claims.
- Company/factory facts: Eastern Camel capability, local factory, local stock, delivery time, capacity, and `15000㎡` factory area.

## How To Use High-Risk Facts

1. If confirmed, use them precisely and cite/source internally when possible.
2. If unconfirmed, omit the claim. A narrower or weaker proposition may be used only when the evidence itself directly supports that proposition and its applicability boundary; changing an exact claim into a generic assertion is not verification.
3. If the user explicitly supplies an unverified high-risk claim as approved copy, keep it only in the internal pending state and exclude it from the finished script until source confirmation exists.
4. If the user asks for "hardcore" or "cold" tone, increase specificity and structure, not unsupported numbers.

## Permitted Claim Scope

- Describe an SBC 801 or life-safety objective only to the strength and scope stated by the verified source.
- Distinguish requirements by verified occupancy, use, area, population, risk classification, drawing, or approval condition; do not imply that every listed variable controls every project.
- State consistency among drawings, material schedules, performance evidence, and installed work only when the applicable review or inspection source establishes that relationship.
- Use an Eastern Camel material or configuration claim only after the company/product landing gate passes and the capability is verified for the relevant product and project condition. Do not infer approval, compliance, or a complete fire-safety solution from availability of one material.
- Factory area and `15000㎡` only fit verified claims about local manufacturing scale, production capacity, or delivery certainty. They do not support pure SBC 801 / Civil Defense education, egress, fire separation, firestopping, or document-consistency claims.

## Competitor / Low-End Market Comparison

Use competitor contrast only as a verified or carefully framed risk pattern.

Allowed comparison scope requires evidence for the compared solution type, relevant occupancy or risk category, and the exact decision dimension. Valid dimensions may include material evidence, assembly details, penetrations, schedules, reports, and installed-work consistency; their presence in this list does not establish a market-wide defect or project outcome.

Avoid unless sourced:

- Claims of widespread corner-cutting or a universal market practice.
- Categorical claims that another solution has no fire performance.
- Exact failure-time claims.
- Claims about a competitor's pricing motive.

## Hard Parameters And Certificates

Use this decision rule:

- If exact parameter/certificate is supplied by the user and marked as confirmed, include it in the hard script.
- If it exists in local files but is marked conflict/pending, include it in "待核实高风险事实" rather than the script.
- If it is only in an accepted style sample, do not treat it as verified.
- If not verified, omit it. Use a requirement direction only when the source itself directly establishes that direction for the applicable scenario; a generic qualifier does not repair missing evidence.

## Required Internal Check

Before returning a final script, internally verify each material claim against its evidence and permitted strength or applicability boundary:

- Identify facts confirmed by local source files.
- Exclude unresolved high-risk claims from the finished script and keep them in the internal pending state.
- Express each claim only at the strongest level the evidence itself supports; if the evidence supports no proposition in that area, omit it.
