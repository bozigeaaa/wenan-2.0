# Hotspot Script Pattern

Read `../../references/generation-trigger-registry.md` before drafting. It is the authority for public generation triggers, information relationships, hook timing, conditional placement, oralization, length review, promise validation, ending conditions, and deduplication. This file adds only the evidence and risk boundaries specific to verified international or Saudi project news.

News may be the entry or the evidence. Explain only an industry impact that has a sourced operational link to the event and the content promise. A selected product or company may appear only when its verified role helps fulfill that promise. Never imply project participation, supply, endorsement, or a commercial relationship without evidence.

## Domain Checks

- Keep event facts, dates, actors, location, and project stage traceable to the news verification card.
- Label inference at its actual strength; do not turn possibility into certainty.
- Product fit must come from a real operational link, not shared industry vocabulary.
- Current inventory, price, lead time, capacity, delivery, installation, and after-sales claims require current company evidence.
