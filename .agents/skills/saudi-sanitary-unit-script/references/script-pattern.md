# Sanitary-Unit Script Pattern

Read `../../references/generation-trigger-registry.md` before drafting. It is the authority for public generation triggers, information relationships, hook timing, conditional placement, oralization, length review, promise validation, ending conditions, and deduplication. This file adds only the evidence and risk boundaries specific to mobile-toilet and sanitary-unit configuration, operation, maintenance, and camp use.

Comparisons among TBOX, K-series, built-in, or other options must use matched configuration and use conditions. Keep product documentation, project requirements, authority rules, and matters needing project confirmation distinct. Product or company content may appear only when a verified feature or responsibility helps fulfill the operating content promise.

## Domain Checks

- Verify dimensions, fixture combinations, packing, container loading, materials, drainage, water, electricity, ventilation, installation, cleaning, and service claims from product evidence.
- Do not promise no odor, no leakage, maintenance-free use, guaranteed approval, or universal capacity.
- Identify who or what creates pressure before assigning the problem to the product.
- Current inventory, delivery, installation, and after-sales arrangements require current confirmation.
