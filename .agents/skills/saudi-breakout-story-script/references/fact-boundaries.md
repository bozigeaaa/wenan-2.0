# Fact Boundaries

## Must Verify

Browse current sources for:

- News and public events.
- Visa, labor, tax, customs, government, or policy changes.
- Current prices, exchange rates, fines, or enforcement actions.
- Public project timelines and public figures.
- Competitor or market trend claims.

Use local knowledge files for:

- Saudi temporary construction/camp industry cognition.
- Company-provided project/product facts when the user explicitly asks to connect the story to business.

## Do Not Invent

- Unsourced personal or client encounters.
- Extreme scams, raids, fines, or shutdowns.
- Worker protests or collective action.
- Religious/cultural claims without context.
- Nationality-wide or culture-wide generalizations.

## Permitted Framing Strength

- Scope an event or practice to the verified project category, actor, location, and time range; do not use a vague project subset to imply prevalence.
- Mention government, PIF, or Aramco systems only when a source identifies the relevant institutional, procurement, or approval difference.
- Use owner, region, and contract conditions only when they materially bound the claim; do not append them as a generic disclaimer.
- Interpret a behavior as project-management logic only when evidence supports the operational mechanism. Do not recast an unsupported cultural claim as business analysis.
