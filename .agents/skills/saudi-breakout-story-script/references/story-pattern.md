# Breakout Story Pattern

Read `../../references/generation-trigger-registry.md` before drafting. It is the authority for public generation triggers, information relationships, hook timing, conditional placement, oralization, length review, promise validation, ending conditions, and deduplication. This file adds only the evidence and risk boundaries specific to Saudi life, culture, industry, and project stories.

Use a scene only when its place, people, action, and timing are sourced or supplied by the user. Any move from a local observation to an industry consequence needs a defensible evidence link. Misunderstanding, reversal, surprise, and product appearance are optional; company or product content may appear only when it helps fulfill the same content promise.

## Domain Checks

- Do not invent first-person experience, dialogue, emotion, local custom, customer behavior, or a project scene.
- Separate a single anecdote from a general cultural or market claim.
- Current events and changing local practices require live verification.
- Avoid exoticizing Saudi life or presenting inference as a national trait.
