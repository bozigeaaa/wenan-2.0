# Product Seeding Fact Rules

## Parameters

Use a parameter only when:

1. It is verified in local source files or user-provided material.
2. It directly answers the script's one buyer decision pressure.

Do not include a true parameter just because it sounds impressive.

## Company And Factory Facts

Use company facts only when they directly answer the script's one buyer decision pressure.

- Factory area and `15000㎡` are only relevant to local manufacturing scale, production capacity, or delivery certainty.
- Do not use factory area as a generic trust line for maintenance, compliance, hygiene, material logic, or general procurement-risk topics.
- Local factory, local stock, local delivery, Chinese coordination, and after-sales response must each answer the current decision pressure; otherwise leave them out.

## Competitor Claims

Browse current sources before mentioning:

- Current Saudi market alternatives.
- Competitor names, prices, material levels, market share, or delivery capability.
- "Most suppliers", "mainstream market", "common local solution".

Prefer comparing solution types rather than attacking competitors.

## Unsafe Sales Claims

Avoid:

- Guaranteed approval, guaranteed no maintenance, guaranteed no odor, guaranteed fire safety.
- "Absolutely", "100%", "permanent", "never".
- Fake customer feedback.
- Unsupported ROI, savings ratio, payback period, or fine avoidance.

## Permitted Claim Strength

- Use directional benefit language only when evidence supports the causal relationship; weaker wording does not substitute for evidence.
- State comparative suitability only against a defined alternative and verified use conditions.
- State lower maintenance, cost, delay, or risk only when the mechanism and relevant boundary are supported; quantify the effect only when the metric is verified.
- Record unresolved configuration, site, or owner variables as missing evidence or an applicability boundary. Do not append a generic qualifier to preserve an otherwise unsupported claim.
