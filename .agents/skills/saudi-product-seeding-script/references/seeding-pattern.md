# Product-Seeding Pattern

Read `../../references/generation-trigger-registry.md` before drafting. It is the authority for public generation triggers, information relationships, hook timing, conditional placement, oralization, length review, promise validation, ending conditions, and deduplication. This file adds only the evidence and risk boundaries specific to buyer pressure, product mechanisms, comparison, and conversion claims.

Compare options only on the same verified cost, scope, service, duration, configuration, or responsibility basis. Lifecycle claims require the relevant purchase, transport, assembly, reuse, maintenance, disposal, and residual-value inputs at their actual evidence strength. A product or company role must materially help fulfill the buying content promise; do not manufacture loss, urgency, scarcity, or a promotional send-off.

## Domain Checks

- Keep price, savings, payback, reuse count, service life, stock, lead time, capacity, and performance claims sourced and conditional.
- Do not invent customer stories, competitor defects, market consensus, or guaranteed outcomes.
- Do not rename a purchase as an asset or investment unless accounting and recovery assumptions are actually supported.
- Keep the decision dimension within the evidence actually available; do not force a fixed checklist count.
